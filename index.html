How it works

FLAMES playfully predicts the relationship between two people! Put the name of a person you'd like (preferably yours) and your potential partner's name. We'll cross out the common letters in your names, count the remaining letters, and use that number to cycle through the outcomes:

  • Friends
  • Lovers
  • Affection
  • Marriage
  • Enemies
  • Siblings
